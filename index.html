About

One lab, many kinds of games

GLITCHED MATRIX Prototype Lab is where we build and connect unusual games instead of forcing every experiment into one genre. Recent work ranges from voxel worlds and autonomous simulations to cyberpunk hacking, fantasy adventures, educational software, ecological observation, and tactical combat.

Current Lab Work

What we have been building lately

These are the projects currently defining the lab. Some are release candidates, some are still evolving prototypes, and some are becoming reusable foundations for future games.

Voxel World

The World Beneath

An expanding voxel world with stacked biomes, mining and building, persistent companions, world-cell streaming, fast block updates, and an increasingly strict rendering-reliability standard.

Education

Osmosis University

A standalone game-development school with 15 subjects, 150 certified lessons, Expert Workspace, R-01, real reference files, and 10 certified student games that appear in a personal Game Test Lab.

Cyberpunk

GHOST SIGNAL: UTOPIA

Andrew exists inside Utopia's network and captures the city building by building while Gleebs counters dangerous intrusion methods with readable security rules, ejection, and persistent lockouts.

Fantasy Adventure

Swords & Sorcery: World of Mythologies

A lost Utopia adventure simulator set in Malor, with quests, roads, wild regions, combat, defensive companions, shoulder and first-person cameras, and region-specific audio and atmosphere.

Tactical Simulation

HEX CONTRACT

Autonomous heroes take on dangerous contracts and sovereign bosses across authored broken-maze battlegrounds, with persistent strain, relationships, equipment choices, destructible routes, and tactical setpieces.

Life Simulation

Catnip Island

A cat-centered island simulation with switchable residents, kittens, daily routines, favorite places, social behavior, playful interruptions, and personalities expressed through how each cat uses the world.

Emergent Ecology

Army Ants

A two-colony survival simulation built around decentralized army-ant behavior, trails, prey, tunnels, bivouac survival, territory pressure, and conflicts that emerge from the colonies' needs rather than scripted war.

Observation Game

Alien Geographic

A field-research game organized into ecological chapters, with camera sites, persistent evidence, environmental cycles, species behavior, and research questions that are resolved from what the player actually observes.

Planetary Exploration

Operation: StarFall

Planetary fieldwork across Saturn's moons using drones, rovers, sampling, missions, resources, transport links, and world-specific environmental profiles rather than treating every destination as the same planet with a new color.
